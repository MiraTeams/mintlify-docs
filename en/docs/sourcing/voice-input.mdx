Voice input lets you describe a candidate search out loud instead of typing it. Mira transcribes what you say into the channel input, so you can review or edit before sending. You can also upload an audio file you already have, and Mira reads it to understand your requirements and gets to work.

![Voice input in MiraDay: the channel input recording speech, with a timer, waveform, and confirm and cancel buttons](/docs/voice-input.png)

---

## What it's good for

- Long requirements, where talking is faster than typing every detail.
- Briefing the way you'd brief a colleague. The natural phrasing usually carries more context than a tidy bullet list.
- Moments when typing is awkward but you don't want to lose the thought.

## How to use it

1. Open a channel and find the microphone button in the channel input.
2. Click the microphone button and start speaking.
3. When you're done, click the confirm button. Mira transcribes the recording and adds the text to the channel input, after anything you've already typed.
4. Review or edit the transcription, then send it as usual.

If you change your mind, click the cancel button to discard the recording. Nothing is sent until you choose to.

Each recording is capped at 5 minutes; when you reach the limit Mira stops automatically and transcribes what it has. For a very long brief, record it in shorter segments, or upload it as a file instead.

## Upload an audio file

Already have a recording? Attach an audio file instead of speaking live: a meeting recording, an intake call with a hiring manager, or interview audio. Mira reads it the way it reads an uploaded JD, works out the requirements from it, and gets to work. Unlike live voice, the content isn't placed in your input box as editable text; Mira takes it in as part of the channel.

1. In the channel input, attach an audio file the same way you'd attach a JD or résumé.
2. Common formats are supported: MP3, M4A, WAV, WebM, and OGG. Each file can be up to 100 MB and up to 2 hours long.
3. Mira reads the audio, understands the requirements in it, and uses them in your channel.

<Note>
Recording laws vary: make sure you have the consent required in your jurisdiction before recording and uploading calls or interviews.
</Note>

## Supported languages

Live voice input uses your MiraDay interface language as a hint for transcription, so it works best when you speak in that language. To dictate in another language, switch the interface language first. Uploaded audio files are transcribed in whatever language they're spoken in: Mira detects it automatically.

## Privacy

- For live voice input, the recording is used only to produce the transcription that fills the channel input, and Mira does not store it.
- Uploaded audio files are stored as channel attachments, like any other file you upload.
- Transcription is handled by a third-party transcription provider.
- The transcribed text becomes part of your channel like any other input. See [Privacy & Compliance](/en/docs/match-quality-and-trust/privacy-policy) for how channel content is handled.
