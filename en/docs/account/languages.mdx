MiraDay's interface and documentation are available in four languages: English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese. On your first visit, MiraDay matches your browser language if it's one of these four; otherwise it shows English.

---

## Set your language

**In the app:** click your **profile** at the bottom-left of the sidebar, then choose **Language**. The change takes effect immediately.

**On miraday.ai:** use the **language selector** at the bottom of the homepage, or at the top of the sign-in page.

## Supported languages

| Language | Code |
|----------|------|
| English | en |
| 日本語 (Japanese) | ja |
| 简体中文 (Simplified Chinese) | zh |
| 繁體中文 (Traditional Chinese) | zh-TW |

## What gets translated, and what doesn't

- **Interface:** all menus, buttons, navigation, and system messages appear in your selected language.
- **Mira's replies:** Mira replies in the language you write in, including languages the interface doesn't offer, and you can switch mid-conversation just by writing in another one.
- **Candidate data:** always shown in its original language, never machine-translated, so names, titles, and companies read exactly as they do at the source, without errors introduced by translation.
- **Always in English:** MiraDay, Mira, MRE, and a few other standard product and technical names.
