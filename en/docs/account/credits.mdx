MiraDay uses credits to meter the work you run in MiraDay, like running a search or unlocking a contact. This page covers what uses credits and where to see your usage.

---

## What uses credits

- **AI model usage**: running AI-powered searches, generating candidate summaries, and other AI work.
- **Talent search**: executing a candidate search across multiple public talent data sources.
- **Contact retrieval**: unlocking a candidate's email or phone (each is a separate action).
- **Contact enrichment**: filling in missing profile details for a candidate or contact.
- **Sandbox operations**: running tasks in MiraDay's AI agent sandbox.
- **Audio transcription**: transcribing an audio file you upload, metered by the length of the audio.

## Seeing how much a channel used

![The Channel Details panel showing the Credits Consumed for that channel](/docs/connectors-email-1.png)

Open a channel's details to see the **Credits Consumed** for that channel. When you unlock a contact's email or phone, Mira confirms that the action used credits.

For overall usage, click the Credits indicator at the top right to open **Credit usage** (the title can vary by organization type): a popover on desktop or a drawer on mobile. It shows used and total Credits, your remaining balance, and the reset date; **View usage** opens the details. Mira warns you when Credits are running low or have run out. Members can view usage, while upgrade or contact actions depend on account permissions.

The **Usage & Billing** section of **Settings** also shows your current plan, Credits used this period and when they reset, and full usage details.

## Plans and pricing

Each plan comes with a monthly credit allowance. If you reach your limit, Mira lets you know.

| Plan | Price | Credits |
|------|-------|---------|
| **Free** | $0 | 4,000 credits / month |
| **Individual** | $199 / month | 25,000 credits / month |
| **Team** | $399 / seat / month (minimum 2 seats) | 45,000 credits / seat / month |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Custom credits and per-user allocation |

Free covers the full sourcing agent, including importing contacts and file management. Individual adds a higher credit allowance and email support. Team adds seat and member management. Team usage analytics, shared Channels, Contacts, and Files, and shared Skills and Connectors are coming soon. Enterprise adds custom credits with per-user allocation; SSO/SAML sign-in, SOC 2 reports, dedicated onboarding, and ATS integration are coming soon. Open the Plans page in MiraDay for the full feature comparison.

Credits and plan contents are indicative and may change over time; your actual entitlements prevail.
