This page covers how to sign in to MiraDay and manage your account. If you're setting up for the first time, start with [Set Up Your Account](/en/docs/get-started/create-your-account).

---

## Ways to sign in

MiraDay uses one combined **Sign in or Sign up** page. Choose **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft** to use an existing account, or enter your email and Mira takes you to the right next step:

- **Email and password.** New to MiraDay? You'll set a password (at least 8 characters, with letters, numbers, and a symbol). Returning? Enter your password. The one-time email code you may see only verifies a new or unverified email address; it isn't a way to sign in.
- **Enterprise single sign-on (SSO).** If your organization uses SSO, entering your work email shows **Enterprise account detected**. Click the **Continue with** button to use your company's sign-in. No separate password needed.

## Resetting your password

Forgot your password? On the password step, click **Forgot password**. Mira emails you a reset link, and you set a new one. This is also how you change your password; there's no separate change-password screen.

## Managing your account

Open the user menu from your profile at the bottom-left of the sidebar. From there you can:

- Switch your **Language**. See [Languages](/en/docs/account/languages) for the full list.
- Open **Settings**, a popup with sections for Account, General, Usage & Billing, Skills, Connectors, and Browser Extension (desktop only). Account is where you update your profile and email, upload an avatar, and delete your account. General holds language and theme (Light, Dark, or System). Team admins manage their team by clicking **Manage team** in **Usage & Billing**.
- **Sign Out**.

Your display name shows on your avatar; you can change it anytime in the **Account** section of **Settings** (**Nickname**).

## Deleting your account

Open **Settings**, go to **Account**, and click **Delete account**. You confirm by typing your email address and then **Permanently delete**; this removes your account and all task data and can't be undone. If you have an Individual subscription, deleting your account cancels it first. If you're the admin of a Team or Enterprise organization, you can't delete your account until the organization is handled; email [support@mira.day](mailto:support@mira.day) for help. Your data is handled as described in the full privacy policy at [mira.day/privacy](https://mira.day/privacy); see also [Privacy & Compliance](/en/docs/match-quality-and-trust/privacy-policy).
