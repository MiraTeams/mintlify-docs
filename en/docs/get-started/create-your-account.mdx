Creating a MiraDay account is open to everyone, no invitation needed. Here's how to sign up and get started.

---

## Step 1: Sign up

Go to [miraday.ai](https://www.miraday.ai) and click **Start for free**. You'll see the **Sign in or Sign up** screen, with three ways to create your account:

- **Continue with your Google account.**
- **Continue with your Microsoft account.**
- **Sign up with your work email.** Enter your email and click **Continue**, then create a password with at least 8 characters, including letters, numbers, and a symbol, and click **Continue** again. If your company uses single sign-on, MiraDay detects it and sends you to your company's sign-in instead.

## Step 2: Verify your email

If you signed up with email, MiraDay sends a verification code to that address. Enter the code to confirm your email. A verification code only confirms your address, it isn't how you sign in later.

## Step 3: Tell us a little about yourself

Before you start, MiraDay asks a few quick questions on one screen:

- **What's your name?** Your first and last name. If you signed up with Google or Microsoft, these are filled in from your account.
- **What best describes your role?**
- **How did you hear about MiraDay?**
- **Add your LinkedIn profile (optional).**

Click **Get started** to finish. If your account is managed by your organization through single sign-on, you skip this step.

## Step 4: Start sourcing

You'll land on the New channel screen, ready to create your first sourcing task.

For later sign-ins, open the [MiraDay sign-in page](https://app.miraday.ai/sign-in).

Sign in the same way you signed up: Google, Microsoft, or your email and password. You'll land on the New channel screen.

## Choose your language

MiraDay is available in multiple languages. Pick your language from the selector on the sign-in screen, or change it anytime from the user menu at the bottom of the sidebar.
