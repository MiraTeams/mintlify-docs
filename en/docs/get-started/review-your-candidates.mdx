After a search, Mira delivers a candidate pool: a ranked set of candidates who match your requirements. Here's how to read it and decide what to do next.

## Where your candidates appear

![The candidate pool in table view, with columns for Candidate, Qualifications, Location, and Job Title, and the colored fit labels](/docs/Candidates-appear.png)

Your search results show up in two places:

- **In the conversation**: Mira summarizes what it found and gives you a downloadable data file.
- **In the right panel**: your candidates, ordered by how well they fit. A toggle at the top of the panel switches between two layouts:
  - **Card view** (the default): candidate cards you can scroll through.
  - **List view**: a compact table with columns for Candidate, Qualifications, Link, Email, Phone, Location, Job Title, Company, School, Degree, and Major, handy for scanning many candidates at once.

## Read your first pool

Each card carries a color-coded fit label, **Good Match**, **Partial Match**, or **Low Match**, next to its **Qualifications** heading, and candidates are ordered by fit, strongest at the top. The Qualifications section checks the candidate against your must-haves one by one, with the evidence behind each judgment.

The label and Qualifications are a fast read, not the whole story. The same title means different things at different companies, so check company context and career trajectory before you decide. [Inside a Candidate Profile](/en/docs/sourcing/candidate-profile) covers every card field and how to evaluate a candidate in depth.

## The data file

Every pool comes with a downloadable data file. Use it to import candidates into your ATS, share them with a hiring manager, or track them in a spreadsheet. [Export & Share](/en/docs/get-started/export-and-share) covers what the file includes and how to export a custom subset.
