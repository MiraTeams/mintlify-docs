MiraDay is a Talent Intelligence platform for professional and leadership hiring. Its agent, Mira, finds you the right candidates with a team of agents: describe a role, and Mira searches, reasons, and brings back the people who actually fit. Every search puts an Agent Team on the job, each teammate owning part of the hunt. You take the volume off your desk; you stay the one who decides.
