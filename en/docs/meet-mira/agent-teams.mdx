With an Agent Team, Mira acts as team lead and brings in teammates, each taking a different part of the task, while you stay in control. Recruiting searches are the most common use, but a team can take on any hard, multi-step job.

---

## A teammate for every part

A hard job has several moving parts. For a search, that often means several sourcing teammates working in parallel, each on its own region or talent segment, with others researching companies or checking facts. The same holds for other multi-step work, like mapping a market or profiling several companies at once.

Every candidate search runs as an Agent Team: Mira always hands the search to at least one sourcing teammate and brings in more when the work needs them. For other tasks, Mira decides; a focused, well-scoped request usually runs with Mira alone.

## The team works together in the channel

A channel is where an Agent Team does its work. Like a real team in a group chat, teammates share what they find, build on each other's work, and challenge each other's conclusions to reach a better result. You can follow along from start to finish, check any step as it happens, and trace the evidence behind every conclusion.

In the **Task workspace** on the right, **Task plan** tracks each stage as it completes, and **Member status** shows what each teammate is doing live.

When the team finishes, Mira pulls every teammate's results together and delivers the final answer to you in the main conversation.

## Who's on the team

When the team starts, Mira posts who it enlisted: "Mira assembled the Agent Team, enlisting N teammates," followed by each teammate's name, role, and what they're responsible for. Mira then posts "Mira invited … to the channel" and lists each teammate's **Assignments**, the specific work for this run.

Teammates work through their part on their own. When a step involves outreach, sensitive data, or a high-risk action, Mira waits for your confirmation first.

## Steer the search as it runs

You confirm the direction rather than micromanage the work. Before the team searches, Mira has you review and confirm an **Ideal Candidate Profile**: your must-haves, nice-to-haves, and things to avoid. Once the team is running, you steer by talking to Mira in the channel, and Mira passes your guidance to the right teammate. You can also edit the profile in the **Ideal Candidate Profile** bar at the top of the results panel and click **Search**. If a search is still running, Mira asks whether to **Wait** or **Start new search**, which stops the current one.

## Starting a team

There are two ways a team starts:

1. **Mira assembles one automatically** for every candidate search, and for any other task that's a better fit for a team.
2. **You turn it on** with the **Agent Team** button in the channel input toolbar. It highlights when active and is remembered per channel. Turn it on to have Mira use a team for other kinds of work too; candidate searches use a team either way.

## Credits

Like other work in MiraDay, an Agent Team run uses credits. You can see how many a channel used in **Channel Details**, under **Credits Consumed**.

## FAQ

### Can teammates use the browser extension?

Yes. If the work needs it, authorize browser control first, a one-time approval you give in the main conversation, and then teammates can browse too. They can't request that authorization mid-run, so turn it on up front. See [Browser Extension](/en/docs/tools-and-teamwork/mira-browser-extension).

### Can I run a search without an Agent Team?

No. Every candidate search runs as an Agent Team, even when it needs only one sourcing teammate. For other tasks, leave the Agent Team button off and Mira decides; a focused request usually runs with Mira alone.
