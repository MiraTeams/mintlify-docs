MiraDay is a Talent Intelligence platform for professional and leadership hiring. It reasons about who actually fits a role, not just whose profile uses the right words. [Why MiraDay](/en/docs/meet-mira/why-mira) explains the difference.

Its lead agent, Mira, works alongside you like a recruiting team. Every candidate search runs as an Agent Team: Mira hands the search to a sourcing teammate, brings in more teammates when the job calls for it, and coordinates the work. Whether a candidate moves forward stays your call.

---

## How MiraDay works with you

Describe the role in plain language, no forms or boolean strings. Mira confirms an Ideal Candidate Profile with you, then searches across many sources, follows and cross-checks the signals over several steps, and returns candidates ordered by relevance, each with source-backed evidence for what's met, what's uncertain, and what's missing. [How MiraDay Works](/en/docs/meet-mira/how-mira-works) walks through the full loop.

## What you can do today

![MiraDay's New channel screen, describe a role in your own words](/docs/task-new.png)

Right now, MiraDay's primary use case is talent sourcing:

- **Describe a role in your own words, or upload a job description:** including implicit criteria like "startup DNA" or "has built something from scratch."
- **Get a candidate pool** with profiles, a Qualifications assessment, and recent work experience. 40 candidates by default, up to 100 if you ask.
- **Refine through conversation:** "more startup backgrounds" or "include Amsterdam too."
- **Reveal contact details:** email and phone on demand (uses credits).
- **Download structured data:** import into your ATS or spreadsheet, or share with your hiring manager or client.
- **Go further** with [Agent Team](/en/docs/meet-mira/agent-teams), [Skills](/en/docs/tools-and-teamwork/skills), and the [Mira Browser Extension](/en/docs/tools-and-teamwork/mira-browser-extension).

## Where do the candidates come from?

| Source | What it covers |
|--------|---------------|
| **Public talent data** | Aggregated from multiple public talent data sources and providers, including LinkedIn, X, GitHub, and Hugging Face, and data providers like Exa, Apollo, and RocketReach. Covers industries, regions, and seniority levels globally |
| **MiraDay's own index** | Continuously updated index of public professional profiles and job market data |
| **Mira Browser Extension** | Navigates recruiting platforms, company websites, forums, and internal systems on your behalf |

## Who uses MiraDay

MiraDay is built for professional and leadership hiring: in-house Talent Acquisition and executive/leadership search teams, and the executive-search and professional recruitment agencies that serve them, on the specialized, business-critical roles where evidence and judgment matter more than application volume.
