Connectors link your own accounts to MiraDay, so Mira can use them while it works in a channel. The one most recruiters reach for first is email: connect your **Gmail** or **Outlook**, and Mira can draft, send, and summarize email as part of a channel, without you switching back and forth to your inbox.

---

## Connect your email

![The connectors menu in the channel input, with Gmail and Outlook ready to connect and a link to Manage connectors](/docs/connectors-email.png)

1. Open **Connectors** from **Settings**, or from the connectors menu in the channel input.
2. Find **Gmail** or **Outlook** and click **Connect**.
3. **Authorize** Mira when prompted. You're sent to sign in and grant access, then brought back.
4. Once connected, your mailbox is ready to use in any channel.

Connected services are listed first. Click a connector to open its detail page, where you can turn each tool on or off; tools marked **Approval** only run after you confirm. **Disconnect** at any time (Mira asks you to confirm) and access is revoked right away; you can reconnect later.

## What Mira can do with your email

Once your mailbox is connected, ask Mira to work with email right inside a channel:

- **Draft.** Mira writes the email in the **Email Canvas**, with **To**, **Cc**, **Bcc**, **Subject**, and **Body** all editable inline, and you can add attachments. Review it and make any changes before it goes anywhere.
- **Send.** When the draft is ready, click **Send**. Mira asks you to confirm once more before anything leaves your mailbox, so nothing is sent until you approve it.
- **Forward.** Forward an email from within a channel. Its original attachments come along; forwarded emails can't take new attachments.
- **Read and summarize.** Point Mira at your inbox to pull in a long thread and get the gist, or catch up on what's come in, without leaving the channel.

## Privacy and security

- Connectors use your own authorization. Mira uses a connected account only to do what you ask inside a channel.
- Nothing is sent without your confirmation, and you can disconnect a connector at any time from the Connectors page; its access is revoked right away.
- All data handling follows MiraDay's [Privacy Policy](/en/docs/match-quality-and-trust/privacy-policy).
