## I can't sign in

- Make sure you're using the email address you registered with, and sign in with your password.
- Forgot your password? Use **Forgot password** to reset it by email.
- If you signed up with Google or Microsoft, use that same button to sign in.
- If your company uses enterprise single sign-on (SSO), enter your work email and continue with your company's sign-in. No MiraDay password is needed.
- If your account has been disabled, the sign-in page says so.
- A verification code, if you receive one, only confirms a new or unverified email address. It isn't how you sign in.

## Mira isn't returning results

- Check that your description is specific enough. Very vague queries (e.g., "find me someone") may not produce useful results.
- Try including more context: role type, skills, experience level, location.
- If a search runs longer than a few minutes, check the channel in the sidebar: a spinner means it's still running, and a dot means it finished. Refresh the page if nothing changes.

## Candidate contact information is missing

- Not all candidates have publicly available email addresses or phone numbers.
- Click **Get phone** to try retrieving a phone number (this uses credits).
- Use the LinkedIn link to reach candidates through InMail.

## The browser extension says it needs an update and I can't skip it

- Your Mira Browser Extension is older than the version Mira requires, so browsing is blocked until you update.
- If the card shows **Update now**, click it to update in one click.
- Otherwise, go to `chrome://extensions/`, turn on **Developer mode**, and click **Update**.
- If it still won't update, click **Remove** on the Mira extension, then use **Open store** on the card and click **Add to Chrome** to reinstall.
- Once you've updated, Mira picks the channel back up.

## The interface is in the wrong language

- Open **Language** from your profile menu (click your name or avatar in the bottom-left), or open **Settings** and go to **General**.
- On miraday.ai, use the language selector at the bottom of the page.

## Something else isn't working

If you're experiencing an issue not covered here:

1. Try refreshing the page.
2. Try signing out and back in.

If the issue persists, reach out to the Mira team for help with a description of what happened.
