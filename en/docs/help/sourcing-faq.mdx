## How many candidates can I get?
By default Mira returns 40 candidates. To get more or fewer, say so when describing the role, for example, "find me 50 ML engineers" or "show me 10 senior PMs", or enter the number in the clarification form, up to 100 per search. You can also ask for more candidates in the same conversation at any time.

## How long does a search take?
Most sourcing rounds complete within a few minutes. Complex searches with very specific criteria may take longer.

## Can I search for candidates in specific countries or cities?
Yes. Include location in your description (e.g., "Berlin or remote", "London", "Southeast Asia"). Mira understands geographic preferences and can filter or prioritize by location.

## What if the results don't match what I need?
Expand the **Ideal Candidate Profile** bar at the top of the results panel, add, edit, or remove criteria, then click **Search** to run a new round of results with the updated profile. You can also tell Mira in the same conversation, for example: "Focus more on startup backgrounds" or "Include candidates from Amsterdam too." Mira adjusts the next search based on your feedback.

## Can Mira work with candidates from my ATS?
You can upload resume files directly, or import an ATS export (CSV or Excel) into [Contacts](/en/docs/tools-and-teamwork/contacts). With the Mira Browser Extension, Mira can also navigate your ATS in your browser using your own login. This is browser automation you control, not a direct ATS integration.

## What does the Qualifications assessment tell me?
The Qualifications assessment is a concise evaluation of how well the candidate fits your search requirements. It opens with a color-coded label, Good Match (green), Partial Match (yellow), or Low Match (grey), then checks the candidate against your must-haves criterion by criterion and highlights relevant experience, skills, and career patterns, not a generic bio.

## Can I download the candidate data?
Yes. Every candidate pool comes with a downloadable data file containing all candidate information in a structured format. You can import this into your ATS or spreadsheets.

## Does Mira remember my preferences across channels?
Within a channel, Mira keeps the full conversation context and refines based on your feedback. Each new channel starts fresh, but all your past channels stay in the sidebar to revisit or continue anytime.
